Signature service

Front & exterior door restoration

$500–$800 per door

This is a restoration and refinishing service — not simply a repaint. Pricing includes preparation, repair of minor surface damage, peeling-paint prep, priming, and two coats of alkyd enamel. Particularly large doors, or doors with a sidelight, are generally toward the upper end of the range.

Two visits are required because the enamel needs an overnight cure between coats. The return visit to rehang the door and reinstall the hardware is included in the price.

Common questions

Pricing questions, answered

How much does it cost to paint a front door in Orlando?

Front door refinishing runs $500–$800 per door. That covers full front door restoration — surface repair, peeling-paint prep, priming, and two coats of durable alkyd enamel — across two visits so the enamel can cure overnight between coats. Oversized doors and doors with a sidelight sit toward the top of the range.

How much does it cost to restore an interior door?

Interior door restoration is $120–$280 per door, depending on condition. Light scuffs are toward the lower end; scratches and chips that need filling and priming fall in the middle; peeling or gouged doors that need real repair land at the upper end.

Why does door restoration cost more than ordinary painting?

Because most of the work happens before the finish coat. A generic painter rolls paint over what's there; door restoration means repairing damage, prepping failing paint, priming, and applying an enamel that holds up to daily use and weather. On exterior doors that also means two visits and an overnight cure. You're paying for preparation and a finish that lasts — not just a fresh coat over an old problem.

What is the minimum project charge?

Every job carries a $350 minimum, whatever the line items add up to. It keeps small, detail-heavy jobs — a single door, a quick trim touch-up — worth doing right.
